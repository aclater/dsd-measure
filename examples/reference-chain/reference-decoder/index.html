The step overshoots by 6.8 percent and settles within 1 percent in 0.32 ms.

Frequencies arrive 0 microseconds apart between 1 kHz and 20 kHz.

Distortion plus noise is -147 dB at 1 kHz and -147 dB at 20 kHz, so the treble is as clean as the midrange.

Quiet sounds keep their level down to -123 dBFS within 0.5 dB.

Across the sweep the worst CCIF figure is -135 dB (pair centred on 19.5 kHz) and the worst SMPTE figure -93 dB; SMPTE rises at low level because the signal gets small.

Tones above the output's own limit leave images no louder than -133 dB relative to the tone (worst at 20.0 kHz); lower is better.

After a 50 ms burst above the working range a quiet tone is back to its normal noise level after 48 ms.

Mixing bass and treble creates under 0.0001 percent unwanted tones (SMPTE test).

Two very high tones leave under 0.0001 percent unwanted tones in the audible band (CCIF test).

A 15 kHz tone: the strongest unwanted tone below 20 kHz is -156 dB down at 19.9 kHz.

File-level J-Test: the largest sideband is -150 dB down (arithmetic only, not a clock test).
