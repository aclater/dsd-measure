Key plots

The treble is flat: the response varies by 0.00 dB from 20 Hz to 20 kHz and is -0.00 dB at 20 kHz.

31 stepped coherent tones at 0 dB SACD, relative to 1 kHz.

The filter is linear phase. Ringing before the main pulse lasts 1.41 ms (more than 0 means a 'pre-echo'), after it 1.41 ms.

Averaged response to 8 impulses at 0 dB SACD, raw output samples.

Background noise is -152 dBFS weighted for hearing (-149 dBFS unweighted); quieter is better.

Welch 8 averages, BH7 window, dBFS per Hz; plotted in 1/48-octave power buckets.

At reference level distortion plus noise is -147 dB (excellent); higher SINAD (147 dB) is better.

Coherent 1 kHz, BH7 window, notch method 20 Hz-20 kHz.

With 32 tones playing at once the cleanest range is 104 dB below the quietest tone; this shows how the device copes with music-like signals.

32 tones, 17.6 Hz-21.4 kHz, crest 13.3 dB, peak at 0 dB SACD.

A 1 kHz tone with total harmonic distortion -164 dB; the biggest unwanted tone is harmonic 3 at -167 dB.

More plots

The step overshoots by 4.5 percent and settles within 1 percent in 0.19 ms.

Frequencies arrive 1 microseconds apart between 1 kHz and 20 kHz.

Distortion plus noise is -147 dB at 1 kHz and -147 dB at 20 kHz, so the treble is as clean as the midrange.

Quiet sounds keep their level down to -123 dBFS within 0.5 dB.

Across the sweep the worst CCIF figure is -134 dB (pair centred on 19.5 kHz) and the worst SMPTE figure -93 dB; SMPTE rises at low level because the signal gets small.

After a 50 ms burst above the working range a quiet tone is back to its normal noise level within about 10 ms.

Mixing bass and treble creates under 0.0001 percent unwanted tones (SMPTE test).

Two very high tones leave under 0.0001 percent unwanted tones in the audible band (CCIF test).

A 15 kHz tone: the strongest unwanted tone below 20 kHz is -156 dB down at 19.9 kHz.

File-level J-Test: the largest sideband is -141 dB down (arithmetic only, not a clock test).

The highest ultrasonic noise peak is -61 dBFS at 280 kHz.

At the loudest allowed level the 28-bit window reaches 78.6 percent modulation; it never saturates.

Method and settings
Profilefull: seg_s 2.5, step_db 3.0, fr_every 1, noise_s 15.0, mt_periods 8, imd_s 4.0, hold_s 60.0, jtest_s 24.0, tone_s 4.0
Analysis64-bit float; 2^18-point FFT at 352.8 kHz bin spacing, 7-term Blackman-Harris window, coherent tones, notch method for THD+N, 20 Hz to 20 kHz, A-weighting per IEC 61672
Reference chainversion 1: the stability-guarded single-bit modulator, then a long linear-phase FIR decoder (160 dB stopband)
